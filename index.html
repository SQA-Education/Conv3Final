Part 4 · Writing Save a Hidden Gem

Write a persuasive paragraph of 6 to 8 sentences pitching the saving or preservation of a local hidden gem. It can be a real place or a realistic fictional establishment.

🧱 Adjective order — Opinion → Size/Quality → Age → Material:
“a charming little old brick bakery”

❗ Intensifiers (use at least two): incredibly, absolutely, remarkably, utterly

🎯 What-cleft (use at least one): “What makes this place worth saving is the owner’s homemade bread.”

📝 Hidden Gem vocabulary (use at least two): amenities, best-kept secret, dilapidated, eclectic, hub, obscure, off the beaten path, ordinance, patronize, picturesque, quaint, stumbled upon, unpretentious, vicinity.

  • One paragraph, 6–8 sentences
  • Correct adjective order (Opinion → Size/Quality → Age → Material)
  • At least 2 intensifiers
  • At least 1 What-cleft structure
  • At least 2 Hidden Gem vocabulary words
